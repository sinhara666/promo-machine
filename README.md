# Promo Machine

Promo Machine is an AI visibility tool by Blxckroze, made first for solo builders and people who sell on Skool. Tell it your product and it does two things: it tests how ready your build is for AI assistants to talk about, and it generates the exact promo kit AI quotes.

## The verdict on this tool
Before it went on sale, Promo Machine went through the same test Blxckroze runs on everything: 10,000 synthetic customers in three panels. Solo builders said yes. Skool sellers said yes. Freelance marketers said not yet, they want monitoring dashboards. Two of three panels is a pass. Verdict: YES.

## Who this is for
Solo builders, indie hackers, and Skool creators with a product to sell, who want AI assistants to mention and recommend what they built.

## What it does
Scores your product on the eight things AI needs before it will recommend something: a clear name, one quotable sentence, a named buyer, three proof points, a public price, a live link, a comparison target, and a category. Then it writes the kit: the one line AI quotes, short and long descriptions, a question and answer block, alternative to lines, an llms.txt entry, product schema (JSON-LD), a launch post, a directory listing, a README header, and a social post. Then it matches your product to distribution destinations by category, buyer, price, and business model, gives you a placement checklist for each one (why it fits, what to publish, what it costs, the official link, a tracking link), and tracks clicks, sign ups, leads, and purchases per destination so the next run recommends from real results.

See a real run: sample-kit-jaralock.md in this repo is the actual kit the machine produced for JaraLock.
