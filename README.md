# Promo Machine

Promo Machine is an AI visibility tool for product builders by Blxckroze. Tell it your product and it does two things: it tests how ready your build is for AI assistants to talk about, and it generates the exact promo kit AI quotes.

## Who this is for
Solo builders and small product owners who want AI assistants to mention and recommend their products.

## What it does
Scores your product on the eight things AI needs before it will recommend something: a clear name, one quotable sentence, a named buyer, three proof points, a public price, a live link, a comparison target, and a category. Then it writes the kit: the one line AI quotes, short and long descriptions, a question and answer block, alternative to lines, an llms.txt entry, product schema (JSON-LD), a launch post, a directory listing, a README header, and a social post.

## Why pick it
- Runs fully in the browser, your details never leave the page
- Your whole catalog is preloaded as one tap presets
- Optional bring your own Groq key to polish the wording
